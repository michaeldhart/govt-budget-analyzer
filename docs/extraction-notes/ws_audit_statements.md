## Validation (per statement per year; all 0 problems)

Method: every printed `Total ...` / `Net cash ...` equals a run of the lines above it starting at a heading (suffix-sum "reduce", `audit_ws_common.reduce_rows`); totals are then replaced by the printed value so higher totals check against totals. Plus statement identities:
- Net position: assets (+ deferred outflows) = liabilities (+ deferred inflows) + total net position (every year; FY2012 both columns).
- Income: revenues, expenses (+ separate depreciation), operating income, non-operating total, income before transfers, change in net position incl. capital grants / contributions / transfers, ending = beginning (restated) + change.
- Cash flows: each section's net = its lines, net change = sum of sections, ending = beginning + net change, reconciliation items = net cash from operations (and equals the statement's figure), noncash / total cash = lines.
- Across: ending net position = total net position; ending cash = cash (+ restricted) in the net position statement; operating income in the reconciliation = income statement; ending net position / GAAP change = the budgetary summary schedule actual column (FY2012-FY2026, all agree).
- Independent second reading: FY2016-FY2026 text layer vs 300 dpi OCR: amounts agree line for line in 7 years; in FY2018, FY2020, FY2021, FY2024, FY2025 one or two OCR misreads differ and the OCR reading fails its own checks while the text reading passes (so the check is meaningful). FY2012-FY2015 are OCR-only (text layers unusable); identities are the validation. Totals checked per statement are in `ws_audit_statements_validation.csv` (e.g. net position 7-11, income 3-4, cash flows 4-7 per year, plus the identities).
- FY2012-FY2015 schedules (`ws_audit_old_schedules_validation.csv`): summary and operating-expense totals tie in all three columns (and the variance and FY2011 columns in FY2012); FY2012 additionally checks the variance of all 97 rows (variance = actual - final, final - actual for expenses); Water / Sewer / Collection system subtotals in the summary equal the expense schedule totals; the actual column (revenues, expenses, operating income, change, ending net position) equals the audited income statement, all years.

Source-arithmetic notes that are NOT problems: FY2020 and FY2021 reconciliation depreciation differs from the income statement by 1,994 / 1,995 (amortization of the asset retirement obligation; each statement ties internally). Cash flow section sums vs net change are `diff` in the summary only for FY2013 and FY2014, because those years print no subtotal for the noncapital and investing sections (a single line each); the net change itself ties.
