# Belvidere, IL budget analysis

Analysis of City of Belvidere tax revenue, starting with FY2027 and extending into year-over-year history. This project was started in a claude.ai chat on 2026-10-09; this file carries that context over.

## Architecture (since 2026-10-22)
Goal: one shared data model for any number of governments, with a custom extraction pipeline per government and an explicit registry of the places where a government's data does not fit the shared model.
```
govbudget/                    shared, entity-agnostic: schema.py (vocabulary + table layouts), entity.py (config/paths/account keys), validate.py
entities/<entity_id>/         hand-edited config, committed: entity.json, categories.csv, account_map.csv, audit_map.csv, comparable_groups.csv, exceptions.csv
pipelines/<entity_id>/        that government's extractors (OCR etc.) and builders; the only code that knows its PDF layouts
data/<entity_id>/             extracted/ (per-year raw extracts), overrides/, then canonical tables: documents.csv, accounts.csv, facts.csv, and derived series
sources/<entity_id>/          source PDFs (older ones are gitignored)
output/<entity_id>/           generated reports
```
- **Canonical tables** (govbudget/schema.py): `facts.csv` is the only place amounts live: one row per source line x period x basis, with `entity_id, doc_id, source_type, period_fy, basis, flow, account_key, label, amount, page_label/pdf_page, flag, exception_ids`. `basis` is one of adopted, prior_doc_budget, revised, actual_prelim, audit_original, audit_final, actual_audited (what the number IS, independent of which document column it came from). `flow` is revenue, expense or contra_expense (a rebate/reimbursement printed in the expense block, e.g. `110-4013` Telecom Tax Rebate, `110-4540`). `documents.csv` has sha256 of each PDF; `accounts.csv` joins the account map to first/last year seen.
- **Mappings are data, not code**: `account_map.csv` (every account the extracts contain -> category and treatment `tax | offset | excluded_fee | non_tax`; 112 rows, 31 are tax-relevant), `audit_map.csv` (audit line label -> comparable group), `comparable_groups.csv` (comparable group -> report group + the budget accounts it contains), `categories.csv` (names, order and colors for charts; two schemes: `budget_category`, `report_group`). Change a classification by editing a CSV, then rerun the builders.
- **Exceptions registry** (`entities/<id>/exceptions.csv`): the sanctioned place for anomalies that do not abstract (kinds: definitional, classification, source_typo, stale_column, omitted_from_total, structural_change, not_comparable, ocr_correction). Scoped to an account (use `a|b` for several), a group, a document or the entity, with a fiscal-year range, an effect (excluded/included/adjusted/informational), a decision date and evidence. Account/document-scoped exceptions are attached to matching rows in `facts.csv` and the series by `exception_ids`; group-scoped ones become the `note` column of `tax_series_audit_basis.csv`. When you find a new anomaly, add a row here (and, if it changes numbers, a rule in the mapping CSVs) instead of burying it in a script or only in this file.
- **Adding a government**: create `entities/<id>/` (entity.json with `fy_start_month` and `account_format`, categories, account_map...), write `pipelines/<id>/` extractors that emit per-document extracts, write a `build_facts.py` that produces documents/accounts/facts in the canonical layout, and run `govbudget.validate`. Everything downstream reads only the canonical tables.
- **Pipeline run order (Belvidere)**: `extract_budget.py FY20XX` and `extract_audit.py FY20XX` per year -> `build_facts.py` -> `build_tax_series.py` -> `build_audit_compare.py` -> `make_tax_report.py` / `make_fy27_pdf.py`. Verified 2026-10-22: after the refactor all derived CSVs and report charts are identical to the pre-refactor outputs (the new columns `entity_id`, `basis`, `is_rollup`, `exception_ids` aside), and re-running the FY2027 budget and FY2026 audit extractors reproduces the committed extracts.
- **Rollup rows**: `tax_series_by_category.csv` still contains `ALL TAXES` rows; they are marked `is_rollup=1` and must be filtered before summing.
- **Not done yet** (next candidates): audited expenses (the audit extractor reads revenues only; the audit reports expenditures by function, a different structure from the budget's department/object lines), other funds (Water/Sewer fund 61 etc. follow the General Fund in the same PDFs), keeping the raw OCR value next to each override-corrected value, a pdf page number on budget rows, curated labels for expense accounts (`account_map.csv` labels come from the latest OCR and have typos such as "Dissburse"), a population/CPI reference table, a regression test suite for the builders.

## Expense extraction (done 2026-10-23, General Fund, budgets FY2015-FY2027)
`extract_budget.py FY20XX` now writes three files per year next to the revenue extract: `FY20XX_budget_gf_expense.csv` (every `01-5-...` expense line, same long format as revenue; 1,720 line items over 13 years, 6,195 amount cells, about 130-140 lines a year), `FY20XX_budget_gf_totals.csv` (every total the city prints: department revenue/expense totals, the Personnel/Contractual/Supplies subtotals, NET lines, fund totals; kinds in `govbudget.schema.TOTAL_KINDS`) and the unchanged revenue file (verified byte-identical for all 13 years). `build_facts.py` turns them into `flow=expense` facts and `control_totals.csv`; `python3 -m govbudget.reconcile belvidere_il` writes `data/belvidere_il/reconciliation.csv` (printed fund totals vs the sum of lines).
- **Validation (every year passes with 0 problems)**: expense rows must sum to every printed subtotal, department expense total, department NET (revenue - expense), TOTAL GENERAL FUND EXPENSES and NET REV OVER (UNDER) EXP, in every column. Expense = expense lines plus the `expense_block_offset` revenue lines (110-4540 Health Insurance Reimbursement is counted as negative expense in the city's subtotals). Revenue and expense sides pick their OCR prep independently (`prep revenue gray, expenses thr150` etc.).
- **Auto-resolve (`autofix` in extract_budget.py)**: before reporting a failure the extractor tries other readings of each cell: the other OCR prep, and the neighbouring year's document (it prints the same budget figure in its prior-year column). A reading is adopted only if it makes the failing printed total tie exactly (one cell, or a pair), within the half-dollar-per-row tolerance for actual columns; ambiguous cases are left alone. A second, weaker tier tries single-digit OCR confusions (1<->4, 6<->8, 5<->6 ...; only for differences of $10 or more) and flags the row `auto_resolved_digit`; the first tier flags `auto_resolved`. Differences of $1 are never auto-resolved (the city's totals are often $1 off). Never changes a cell twice; rechecks everything after each fix. In the final extracts 16 expense lines (63 cells) carry `auto_resolved` (none needed the weaker digit tier); 8 lines have eyeballed row overrides and 1 an `add_row`, all from page images, in `data/belvidere_il/overrides/`; 17 lines are flagged `not_in_printed_total`.
- **Independent cross-check**: each document's prior-year expense column equals the previous year's request column for every shared account in FY2016-FY2022, FY2024 and FY2027 (125-135 cells a year, 0 mismatches). FY2023, FY2025 and FY2026 have 15 mismatches in total (restated prior-year budgets; both documents' printed totals tie): exceptions `EX-restated-prior-expense-fy*`.
- **What the city's printed totals do NOT tie to** (all in exceptions.csv, reconciliation.csv shows them): the Gen Admin total leaves out Heritage Days/ARPA transfer/seized-vehicle lines in some actual columns (`EX-omitted-lines-gen-admin`); the Strolls expense lines are left out of the fund expense total in FY2021-22 columns; the fund expense total is $5,050 above its lines in FY2021 budget and FY2021-FY2024 actual columns (`EX-fund-expense-5050`, not located; every department NET ties); the FY2024 ordinance's Gen Admin personnel subtotal is stale by $76,770; $1 rounding differences. For these, overrides `not_in_total` (value `fund` = only the fund total omits the line) and `allow_diff` record the city's arithmetic and the extractor still passes.
- **OCR quirks found**: `§` read for `5` in account numbers (`01-§-220-7040`), a leading pipe on lines (`| STROLLS NET`), `STROLLS NET` has no hyphen, seven-digit totals that lose a comma (`7,224 853`), `297 ,036`, `Personne!`, an OCR-dropped row (FY2026 Police Grant Expenses, restored by `add_row`; the 0|0 values follow from the totals tying), the Strolls Supplies account read as 7900 (it is 7040), leading 1 read as 4 and 6 read for 5 in large values (hidden when both preps agree; found by the printed totals).
- **Account model**: `account_map.csv` now has 264 rows (112 revenue, 152 expense; expense `treatment` is `expense`); expense categories are object-code ranges (`categories.csv` scheme `expense_object`: personnel 5xxx, contractual 6xxx, supplies 70xx-78xx, miscellaneous 79xx, capital projects 80xx, equipment/vehicles/grant expense 82xx-83xx, transfers 9xxx, exception `EX-expense-categories`); `departments.csv` lists the 20 department codes with a function group (general government, public safety, public works, community development, health and welfare, capital projects and transfers). Accounts are keyed department + object code, as for revenue.
- **Headline** (adopted budgets, printed fund totals): General Fund expenses FY2015 $16.5M ... FY2026 $25.2M, FY2027 $36.2M, of which $10.88M is an operating transfer out of reserves for capital projects (the ordinance says it is a balanced operating budget). The FY2027 revenue is $25.83M.

## Facts about the source documents
- All city budget/audit PDFs are scanned images (no text layer). Extraction needs OCR or reading page images.
- Budget ordinances list three columns: actual for two years prior, prior-year budget, and the current budget request (e.g. FY27 doc has FY25 Actual, FY26 Budget, FY27 Budget Request). Exhibit A is pages "1 of 15" through "15 of 15".
- Revision 780H only changed one salary (part-time building inspector to full-time, $78,000). It states the rest of 753H is unchanged, so 753H is the revenue source of truth for FY2027.
- General Fund total revenues FY27: $25,830,395. Taxes are ~81% of it.
- Account numbers (e.g. `01-4-110-4100` state income tax) are the stable key for matching lines across years. `01-4-...` = General Fund revenue; `01-5-...` = General Fund expense.

## Methodology decisions (keep consistent across years)
- Pie and totals use GROSS tax amounts. Offsets (sales tax and RE tax rebated to developers; replacement tax distributed to pensions) are recorded but shown separately as gross vs. net.
- Fees are not taxes: franchise fees are excluded. The Municipal Infrastructure Maintenance line (`01-4-110-4013`, $62,000 in FY27, $197K in FY14) IS counted as a tax, in Other taxes (user decision 2026-10-22; the city's audits call it "Telecommunications tax"). Open item: `01-5-110-4013` Telecom Tax Rebate (FY2015-19 budget documents, at most $10,783, expense block, not in the printed revenue total) is the rebate of that tax; it is left out of the series, not treated as an offset.
- Scope is the General Fund only. Other funds also receive taxes (police/fire pension property and replacement tax, state motor fuel tax fund, TIF, SSA, capital fund property tax) but are excluded because the pension levies equal the pension expense lines in the police/fire budgets and would double-count.
- Category grouping: Property tax = all 12 RE levies across departments; Other taxes = local motor fuel, cannabis, local use, auto rental, hotel/motel, municipal infrastructure maintenance (telecom).
